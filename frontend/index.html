How the Platform Works — 6-Stage Plain-Language Blueprint

The platform converts operational constraints, costs, and resources into mathematical decision models.

01. TELL US WHAT TO DECIDE
Production, staffing, logistics, inventory or pricing problem.
02. TELL US YOUR RULES
Products, workers, machines, costs, stock limits & deadlines.
03. MODEL COMPILED
Business rules become decision variables x and constraints Ax ≤ b.
04. FEASIBLE SEARCH
Solver evaluates candidate plans and finds the optimal solution.
05. UNDERSTAND RESULTS
See expected profit, cost, waste, utilization & binding bottlenecks.
06. APPROVE DECISION
Operations manager reviews rationale and records decision approval.
